Answer

The Blood of Dawnwalker supports Xbox Play Anywhere.

Context

Xbox’s official preview states that players can access the game on Xbox console and Xbox on PC at no additional cost under Play Anywhere.

This distinction matters because pre-release summaries often merge confirmed facts with expected genre conventions.

Use in planning

Use the same Microsoft account and a supported digital entitlement when moving between the two Xbox ecosystems.

Verification status

Physical discs and purchases from unrelated storefronts do not automatically imply Play Anywhere ownership.

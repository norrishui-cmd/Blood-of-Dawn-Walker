What Is the Blood Tax?

Officially sourcedConcrete answer

Direct answer

The blood tax is the price Brencis demands from human communities in exchange for vampiric protection.

The prologue preview describes villagers giving blood after Brencis saves Lunka and establishes a new order in the plague-stricken valley.

How to use this information

The blood tax frames Brencis as both protector and predator, complicating a simple good-versus-evil reading of his rule.

Evidence boundary

Collection schedules, gameplay penalties, and player choices around the tax are not fully disclosed.

Lore versus outcome

This page records background that official material establishes before release. It can explain motives, institutions, and historical context, but it cannot predict the result of Coen’s choices. Future updates should keep a clean separation between codex-level history, dialogue claims made by characters, and consequences that players can independently reproduce in the final game.

Are Some Quests Limited to Day or Night?

Yes. Some quests and areas are accessible only in Coen’s human or vampire form.

Officially sourcedConcrete answer

Direct answer

Yes. Some quests and areas are accessible only in Coen’s human or vampire form.

Because Coen is human by day and vampire by night, form access can determine when a narrative thread or location can be pursued.

How to use this information

When routing objectives, pair the hourglass cost with the required form instead of treating all tasks as available at all times.

Evidence boundary

A full day-only and night-only quest list is not available before launch.

Planning rule

Apply the are some quests limited to day or night? answer together with three checks: which form Coen currently has, how many hourglass units the next action shows, and which other thread would be delayed. Free exploration and charged quest actions are different categories, so route advice should never deduct time merely because a player walked, searched, or opened the map.

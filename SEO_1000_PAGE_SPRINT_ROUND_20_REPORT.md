# Round 20: 1000-Page Sprint — Multilingual Deepening

Date: 2026-07-29

## Outcome

- Indexable URLs increased from 551 to 614.
- Forty-eight source-checked detail pages were added across Spanish (Spain), French, Italian and Polish.
- Fifteen localized navigation hubs were added for French, Italian and Polish.
- The 165 legacy routes that still lack concrete answers remain `noindex` and outside every sitemap.

## Content clusters

- Four-direction combat and block stamina.
- Shadowstep and wall traversal.
- Infamy and Vrakhiri blood convoys.
- Blacksmith death consequences and broader NPC content loss.
- Coen's family, Anca and Brencis.
- Dynamic weather and visibility.

## Language and design controls

- Every new detail page has a localized direct answer, confirmed facts, decision guidance, a verification boundary, source, visible FAQ, FAQ schema and related guides.
- Every new route uses the English site's shared stylesheet, article hero, verification box, two-column body, sidebar, card grid, FAQ component, footer and responsive rules.
- French, Italian and Polish now have localized Release, Gameplay, Walkthrough, Story and Technical hubs instead of navigation links falling back to a simplified homepage.
- English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish and Japanese equivalents use reciprocal `hreflang` where the same topic exists.

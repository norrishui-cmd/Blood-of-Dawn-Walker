Verified lore answer

Before becoming a vampire, Brencis joined the Roman Senate and rose through official life with the ambition of becoming consul.

Biography and political context

  • His human life was defined by political advancement.
  • Vampirism interrupted rather than created his desire to rule.
  • He later becomes leader of the Vrakhiri in Vale Sangora.

How to read this in the game

Read his Vale Sangora regime as an extension of an ancient political project, not merely a monster occupying a castle.

Evidence boundary

The Vrakhiri are presented as historical individuals from different centuries and cultures, not a single anonymous enemy type. Their biographies explain the regime Coen opposes and help readers distinguish confirmed backstory from conclusions that will depend on dialogue, notes, and endings in the finished game.

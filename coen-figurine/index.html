Coen Figurine Size and Edition

Verified status

The Collector’s Edition includes a 23 cm PureArts Coen figurine in a collector box.

What the source establishes

The figurine is a physical Collector’s Edition item and is not part of Standard, Eclipse, or Day 1 Edition.

Why it matters

Use the published 23 cm size when planning display space and comparing the physical package.

Do not infer beyond this

Final packaging dimensions, weight, shipping cost, and regional stock differ by seller.

Buying check

Compare the coen figurine size and edition answer against four separate needs: the playable base game, digital lore or music extras, physical collectibles, and the pre-order armor early unlock. The armor can be obtained later in-game, so it should not be described as permanently exclusive. Confirm platform, code or disc format, retailer participation, shipping, and local price before ordering.

Spanish (Latin America) Language Support

The Blood of Dawnwalker supports Spanish (Latin America) interface text and subtitles, but no full Spanish (Latin America) audio is listed.

Officially sourcedConcrete answer

The short version

The Blood of Dawnwalker supports Spanish (Latin America) interface text and subtitles, but no full Spanish (Latin America) audio is listed.

Published evidence

The official storefront language table separates text localization from the six languages receiving full voice acting.

Player takeaway

Players can use Spanish (Latin America) menus and subtitles while selecting one of the available spoken-language tracks.

Known limit

Platform-specific download sizes and whether audio can be installed separately are not yet documented.

Before choosing a platform

Language tables describe localization coverage, not necessarily the download workflow. After installation, check the storefront region, game properties, first-launch language menu, subtitle toggle, and spoken-language selector. Do not assume that localized interface text includes voice acting; the full-audio column is the deciding field for Spanish (Latin America) dialogue.

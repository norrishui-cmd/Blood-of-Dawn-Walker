Day 1 Edition Contents

What is confirmed

The Day 1 Edition includes the base game, a SteelBook, a physical world map, and eligible pre-order armor early unlock. It is the physical launch package positioned between Standard and Collector’s Edition contents.

Why players search for this

Choose it when the steelbook and map matter but the figurine and hardcover compendium do not.

What this page does not claim

Retailer stock and participating pre-order bonuses vary.

Buying check

Compare the day 1 edition contents answer against four separate needs: the playable base game, digital lore or music extras, physical collectibles, and the pre-order armor early unlock. The armor can be obtained later in-game, so it should not be described as permanently exclusive. Confirm platform, code or disc format, retailer participation, shipping, and local price before ordering.

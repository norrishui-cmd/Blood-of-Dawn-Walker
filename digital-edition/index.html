Answer

The officially presented digital choices are the Standard Edition and Eclipse Edition.

Context

Eclipse adds a digital world compendium, digital comic book, and digital soundtrack to the base game.

This distinction matters because pre-release summaries often merge confirmed facts with expected genre conventions.

Use in planning

Compare the digital extras rather than expecting physical items such as the steelbook or printed map.

Verification status

Exact storefront availability and local prices can differ by region.

Buying check

Compare the digital edition options answer against four separate needs: the playable base game, digital lore or music extras, physical collectibles, and the pre-order armor early unlock. The armor can be obtained later in-game, so it should not be described as permanently exclusive. Confirm platform, code or disc format, retailer participation, shipping, and local price before ordering.

What the announcement establishes

  • Vale Sangora remains the setting of the September 2026 game.

Correct interpretation

Do not label any real-world country or culture as the next setting until the studio identifies it.

Scope boundary

Behind-the-scenes material can answer who contributed to a feature and what the studio intends, but it should not be mistaken for a launch-build walkthrough.

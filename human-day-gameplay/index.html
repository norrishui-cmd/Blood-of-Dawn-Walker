Answer

Coen is human during the day, using human abilities, sword combat, magic, and access paths distinct from his vampire form.

Context

Day and night are designed as two different gameplay loops with different mysteries and ways to reach objectives.

This distinction matters because pre-release summaries often merge confirmed facts with expected genre conventions.

Use in planning

Schedule human-only areas and conversations during the day, then compare their time costs with night alternatives.

Why Is Svartrau Rich?

Direct answer

Svartrau became wealthy through Vale Sangora’s silver trade.

The capital sits above underground cellars, caves, and ancient ruins, creating a contrast between prosperous streets and hidden dangers beneath them. The city guard also struggles to catch thieves using routes below the streets.

How to use this information

Silver links the city economy to Coen’s mining childhood and the wider history of the valley.

Evidence boundary

Prices, merchant inventories, mine ownership, and economic simulation rules are not public.

Lore versus outcome

This page records background that official material establishes before release. It can explain motives, institutions, and historical context, but it cannot predict the result of Coen’s choices.

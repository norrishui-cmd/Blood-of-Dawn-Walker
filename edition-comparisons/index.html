What this hub covers

Edition pages are purchase aids, so the useful answer is an exact inventory and a clear distinction between physical, digital, and early-unlock content. Regional stock and retailer pricing remain volatile; the official comparison page is the authority for current availability.

This collection is intentionally narrow.

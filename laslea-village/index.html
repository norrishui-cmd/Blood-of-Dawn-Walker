Laslea Village Guide

Officially sourcedConcrete answer

What is confirmed

Laslea is Coen’s home village in the hills of Vale Sangora. The secluded settlement suffered heavily during the Black Plague and was ruled by Skender Dragosti before the vrakhiri takeover. Coen, his parents, and three siblings live there.

Why players search for this

Use Laslea as the anchor for Coen’s family, Zdislav, Anca, the prologue, and early village relationships.

What this page does not claim

A complete street map, merchant list, collectible checklist, and post-prologue state require release-game verification.

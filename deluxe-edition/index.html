Buying check

Compare the is there a deluxe edition? answer against four separate needs: the playable base game, digital lore or music extras, physical collectibles, and the pre-order armor early unlock. The armor can be obtained later in-game, so it should not be described as permanently exclusive. Confirm platform, code or disc format, retailer participation, shipping, and local price before ordering.

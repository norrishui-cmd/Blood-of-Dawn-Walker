Execution Moves Explained

Officially sourcedConcrete answer

The short version

Execution moves are powerful combat actions tied to activation charges.

Published evidence

They sit beside Hex magic in the human combat resource loop, rewarding successful engagement before a high-impact spend. Charges are earned through active combat rather than supplied as an unlimited resource.

Player takeaway

Track charge generation and enemy state rather than treating executions as free finishing animations.

Known limit

Trigger thresholds, invulnerability, boss rules, and damage values need final-game evidence.

Build and testing checklist

At launch, test execution moves explained in both ordinary encounters and restricted situations before recommending a build. Record unlock requirements, resource cost, valid targets, animation commitment, form and time restrictions, upgrades, and failure cases. Official feature confirmation is enough for this overview, but it is not enough for damage rankings or “best” claims.

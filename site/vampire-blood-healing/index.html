Can Vampire Blood Heal Humans?

Yes. Official lore says vampire blood has healing properties for humans.

Officially sourcedConcrete answer

What is confirmed

Yes. Official lore says vampire blood has healing properties for humans. Brencis gives his blood to plague-infected Lunka, and the official Coen family bulletin says the treatment does not appear to create another lasting effect, while deliberately leaving room for uncertainty.

Why players search for this

This explains how Brencis turns healing into political leverage during the Black Plague.

What this page does not claim

The source does not establish a reusable player healing item or guarantee Lunka’s long-term story outcome.

Lore versus outcome

This page records background that official material establishes before release. It can explain motives, institutions, and historical context, but it cannot predict the result of Coen’s choices. Future updates should keep a clean separation between codex-level history, dialogue claims made by characters, and consequences that players can independently reproduce in the final game.

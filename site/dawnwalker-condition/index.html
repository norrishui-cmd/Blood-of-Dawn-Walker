What Is a Dawnwalker?

Officially sourcedConcrete answer

Verified status

A Dawnwalker is Coen’s incomplete vampire condition: human by day and vampire by night, with daylight causing him no harm.

What the source establishes

His form changes the abilities, traversal options, mysteries, and routes available during each half of the cycle.

Why it matters

The condition is the foundation for the game’s two gameplay loops and for form-restricted quests.

Do not infer beyond this

The complete origin, cure possibilities, and ending variations are not disclosed.

Lore versus outcome

This page records background that official material establishes before release. It can explain motives, institutions, and historical context, but it cannot predict the result of Coen’s choices. Future updates should keep a clean separation between codex-level history, dialogue claims made by characters, and consequences that players can independently reproduce in the final game.

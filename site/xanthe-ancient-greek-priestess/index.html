Verified lore answer

Xanthe is the oldest member of Brencis's group and was once a priestess in Ancient Greece; her original name has been lost over the centuries.

Biography and political context

  • She hears voices from beyond the pale.
  • Her age has affected both body and mind.
  • Official biography describes her insight as real rather than simple insanity.

How to read this in the game

Her sparse dialogue should be read as ancient perspective and fear, while remaining alert to how the finished story frames the voices she hears.

Evidence boundary

Hungarian Language Support

The Blood of Dawnwalker supports Hungarian interface text and subtitles, but no full Hungarian audio is listed.

Officially sourcedConcrete answer

What is confirmed

The Blood of Dawnwalker supports Hungarian interface text and subtitles, but no full Hungarian audio is listed. The official storefront language table separates text localization from the six languages receiving full voice acting.

Why players search for this

Players can use Hungarian menus and subtitles while selecting one of the available spoken-language tracks.

What this page does not claim

Platform-specific download sizes and whether audio can be installed separately are not yet documented.

Before choosing a platform

Language tables describe localization coverage, not necessarily the download workflow. After installation, check the storefront region, game properties, first-launch language menu, subtitle toggle, and spoken-language selector. Do not assume that localized interface text includes voice acting; the full-audio column is the deciding field for Hungarian dialogue.

Source

Official Steam store listing. Checked 2026-07-25. This guide separates published facts from details that still require the release build.

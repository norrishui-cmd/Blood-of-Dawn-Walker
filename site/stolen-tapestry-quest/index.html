Quick answer

The official PlayStation description names an opening opportunity to help a weaver by searching for a stolen tapestry commissioned by the Vrakhiri.

Where it fits in the first day

The opening day in Laslea is not a conventional checklist. Exploration itself does not move the clock, but committing to meaningful actions can consume one or more of the eight daytime segments. The practical value of this page is therefore route awareness: know what the activity proves, what it may cost, and what remains impossible to optimize before the retail build is available.

Confirmed sequence

  • The activity is available during Laslea's first day.
  • It competes with family, medicine, and rebellion threads.
  • Its commission connects ordinary village work to Vrakhiri rule.

Route decision

Treat it as a known route candidate, not a finished walkthrough; compare its visible hourglass cost when the retail build launches.

Because the opening segment exposes its time costs before commitment, the reliable launch-week method is to note the hourglass icons, save the resulting dialogue state, and compare only branches actually tested. A preview mention confirms the activity exists; it does not justify inventing a reward table.

What is not confirmed

The thief, recovery steps, rewards, and consequences have not been officially detailed.

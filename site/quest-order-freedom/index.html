Can You Choose the Quest Order?

Yes. The narrative sandbox allows players to choose which threads and vrakhiri targets to pursue first.

Officially sourcedConcrete answer

What is confirmed

Yes. The narrative sandbox allows players to choose which threads and vrakhiri targets to pursue first. Xbox’s preview describes three principal lieutenants with associated stories and says their order is up to the player.

Why players search for this

Use order freedom to investigate the target that best fits the current clues, form, relationships, and time budget.

What this page does not claim

The exact lockouts and dependency graph must be tested in the released game.

Planning rule

Apply the can you choose the quest order? answer together with three checks: which form Coen currently has, how many hourglass units the next action shows, and which other thread would be delayed. Free exploration and charged quest actions are different categories, so route advice should never deduct time merely because a player walked, searched, or opened the map.

Xbox Wire hands-on preview. Checked 2026-07-25. This guide separates published facts from details that still require the release build.

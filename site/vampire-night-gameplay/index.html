Vampire-by-Night Gameplay

Verified status

Coen becomes a vampire at night, gaining claws and supernatural movement options.

What the source establishes

Night gameplay emphasizes powers such as wall movement, rapid traversal, feeding, and routes unavailable to human Coen.

Why it matters

Plan night windows around form-exclusive access rather than merely expecting harder enemies after sunset.

Do not infer beyond this

The complete night ability tree, hunger penalties, and quest list remain incomplete before launch.

Build and testing checklist

At launch, test vampire-by-night gameplay in both ordinary encounters and restricted situations before recommending a build. Record unlock requirements, resource cost, valid targets, animation commitment, form and time restrictions, upgrades, and failure cases. Official feature confirmation is enough for this overview, but it is not enough for damage rankings or “best” claims.

Purchase answer

No. The Sangoran Wayfarer's Armor Set is an early unlock: the official page says every player can obtain it later in the game.

Included-content check

  • It is offered as a digital preorder bonus at participating retailers.
  • The armor features the emblem of a travelling nameless smith.
  • Preordering changes access timing, not permanent ownership eligibility.

Who this option is for

Do not pay a premium solely from fear of missing the armor forever; compare convenience, retailer stock, and the edition's other contents.

Before you order

Edition pages are purchase aids, so the useful answer is an exact inventory and a clear distinction between physical, digital, and early-unlock content. Regional stock and retailer pricing remain volatile; the official comparison page is the authority for current availability.

Check the selected country, platform, format, seller, delivery terms, and final cart description. A global comparison confirms the product structure but cannot guarantee that every edition remains in stock in every market.

What is not confirmed

The normal in-game unlock method and timing are not yet revealed.

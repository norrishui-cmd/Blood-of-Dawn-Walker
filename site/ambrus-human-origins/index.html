Verified lore answer

Ambrus is the youngest vampire in the clique and came from a painful, humble human life marked by humiliation.

Biography and political context

  • He deliberately sought vampiric power.
  • He wants to prove himself to older peers.
  • His polished noble appearance contrasts with his origins.

How to read this in the game

Expect his charm and social presentation to serve ambition; do not assume apparent refinement means loyalty or restraint.

Evidence boundary

What is confirmed

Shrike’s Crag is the site of a massacre ordered by Skender Dragosti after a peasant rebellion. Surviving rebels were trapped at the old quarry and denied mercy. The bloodshed left the place cursed in local memory, and travelers are said not to return.

Why players search for this

This history explains why the Crag is feared before any player reaches it.

What this page does not claim

The official story does not yet confirm a boss, ghost mechanic, quest reward, or exact route there.

What is confirmed

Vale Sangora reacts to Coen’s actions and inaction, not only to major ending decisions. Official features say relationships, power balance, available truths, and the world itself can change as players choose whom to help or ignore.

Why players search for this

This makes consequence tracking useful at the quest and relationship level, not just at a final decision screen.

What this page does not claim

The exact persistence rules and reversible choices are not fully disclosed.
